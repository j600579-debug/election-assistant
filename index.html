Election Process Path

1

Schedule Announcement

ECI declares election dates.

2

Notification Issue

Official call for candidates.

3

Nominations

Candidates file papers.

4

Scrutiny

Verification of documents.

5

Withdrawal

Last date to cancel nomination.

6

Campaigning

Parties meet voters (ends 48hrs before poll).

7

Polling Day

Citizens cast their votes.

8

EVM Sealing

Safe storage of machines.

9

Counting Day

Votes are counted under security.

10

Results

Winners are officially declared.
